1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Bits Weavers Enterprises ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with our Android application Business Calculator ("the App").

Business Calculator is a smart cash-register-style calculator designed to streamline sales, costs, and retail computations. The App is designed to work strictly offline and prioritizes your privacy by keeping your data on your device.

Key Summary: We do NOT collect, store, or have access to any personal information about you. All your business data stays on your device.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not require account creation and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, or any other personally identifiable information.

3. Locally Stored Data

All data you enter is stored locally on your device using SQLite Database and SharedPreferences:

  • Items, prices, costs, and sales entries you create
  • App preferences and settings
  • Backup files you create for export/import

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it. You can voluntarily share data generated by the App as a PDF or printed document. This action is initiated and controlled entirely by you.

4. App Permissions

BizCalculator Demo does not request sensitive Android permissions such as Camera, Microphone, Location, Contacts, SMS, or Phone. It only uses basic storage access to save your local database and PDF backups on your own device.

5. Data Sharing and Selling

We do NOT share, sell, rent, or transfer your personal data to any third party, because we do not collect it.

6. Data Security

Since all business data is stored locally, its security depends on your device security. We recommend using a device lock and keeping backup files secure. We follow standard local storage best practices.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is safe for anyone to use and is intended for a general audience. Since we do not collect personal information from any user, we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained on your device until you delete it inside the App or uninstall the App. Uninstalling will permanently delete the local database unless you have exported a backup. As we have no server copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our end.

9. Your Rights and Control

  • View, edit, and delete all entries inside the App
  • Export data as backup and import it again
  • Delete all data by clearing App data in Android Settings or uninstalling

10. Offline Functionality

The App works strictly offline. All core calculator, itemized display, price look-up, and record-keeping features work without internet.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new policy on this page and update the Effective Date. Please review it periodically.
